## 3. Provisional per-day detail
Format: inventory change / knowledge gained / current goal at end / unresolved threats at end. Items marked (new) are PROPOSALS that appear only if the outline keeps them.

**Day 1 (Episode 1)** - inventory: + 2 bottles of water, + 3 cans (mall); the backpack gets caught on a railing and a minor item may be lost (undecided; default: nothing lost). Knowledge: the Three Rules; mall and stairwell are traps; crowds attract zombies; the utility building is quiet. Goal: survive until morning, then act on the broadcast. Threats: zombies outside the shelter; the evacuation message is unverified.

**Day 2 (Episode 2)** - inventory: nothing important gained; water and food consumed on the road. Knowledge: the evacuation message (location and cut-off time); the road to the bridge is blocked/overrun (default outcome, see unresolved decision). Goal: return to a known shelter with what is left. Threats: low water; a group of zombies now between him and the bridge.

**Day 3 (Episode 3)** - inventory: + 1.5 L water from a building water tank/heater (PROPOSAL), + a metal can opener (new, PROPOSAL, only if cans were an issue). Knowledge: tap water is off; boiling/filtering is impossible; stored water exists in buildings. Goal: find food. Threats: food at zero.

**Day 4 (Episode 4)** - inventory: none gained. Knowledge: fear is a risk factor; a night of noise outside (zombies passing) taught him to stay still. Goal: leave to find food next day. Threats: weakness from hunger; the shelter is known to nearby zombies if noise continues.

**Day 5 (Episode 5)** - inventory: + 4 meals of food, + 1.0 L water, + bandage kit (new, PROPOSAL), + a hand-drawn map or street map (new, PROPOSAL). Knowledge: a trace of another living person (a closed door with a fresh mark / a note) - NOT the stranger himself. Goal: find who left the trace. Threats: the route home was noisy.

**Day 6 (Episode 6)** - inventory: + 1.0 L water and + 2 meals from the stranger (a conditional trade, PROPOSAL). Knowledge: the stranger knows a better building; the stranger's single concrete fact (PROPOSAL, e.g. a skill or an item). Goal: decide whether to follow. Threats: trust is untested.

**Day 7 (Episode 7)** - inventory: + water cache and + 3 meals at the new shelter (PROPOSAL); the survivor leaves the utility building (Episode 1 shelter is abandoned). Knowledge: shared rules; the new shelter's two exits. Goal: hold the shelter and plan Day 8+. Threats: a large group nearby (hook for the next arc); the broadcast is silent.
