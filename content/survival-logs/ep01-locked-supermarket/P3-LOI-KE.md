### 0 · Nhắc lại (20 s, bỏ khỏi Full Movie)

My flare had brought a crowd to our doors.

### 1 · Ngày 11: lên gác
[HUD DAY 11 · WATER 24 L · p3-01]
We braced the damaged landing above the stockroom and tied the ladder to its steel frame. I went first. Maya followed.
[p3-02]
Up there, a metal walkway ran between the air conditioning units.
And at the end of it, a sleeping bag. Water bottles. Crayon drawings taped to a duct.
A bakery jacket lay beside it. Far too big for a child. The sleeves had been rolled up.
[p3-03]
Then I saw her eyes.
[p3-04]
She backed away from me so fast she almost hit the railing.
Not from Maya. From me.

### 2 · Maya nói chuyện
[p3-05]
I stayed by the hatch. Maya knelt down and talked to her for a long time. Quietly.
Her name was Nia. She was nine.
[p3-06 · hồi tưởng]
Her dad drove the bread van. That first night, she'd been riding along because there was no one to watch her.
He knew the maintenance route from his deliveries. When it started, he took her upstairs through the dock. He left food, two cases of water, and his flashlight.
He told her he was going to find help. She had to wait until he came back.
That was why he'd looked back at the dock. She was still inside.
NIA: He said he'd come back.
NIA: Dad said to leave money for anything I took. If I didn't have enough, I had to write it down. He'd pay when he came back.
[p3-07 · hồi tưởng]
She came down through the panel behind the checkout. The narrow aisle along the east wall took her to the shelves.
Our can alarms never crossed it.
She'd taken the crackers and the batteries on different days.
On the fifth night, she brought them back down, and scanned them together.
The coins beside the scanner were hers. So was the folded note.
Nia didn't know the clock was broken. She pressed the unpaid-ticket button. Every receipt said three twelve.
NIA: I didn't want him to come back and think I'd stolen anything.

### 3 · Vì sao cô bé sợ anh
[p3-08 · hồi tưởng]
Then Maya asked why she'd never come to us.
The loft ran over the office corridor. Through a gap, she'd watched me slide the latch shut.
NIA: I thought you'd lock me in too.
[p3-09]
She'd seen me do it. There was nothing I could say to change that.
I set a bottle of water on the walkway, and stepped back.
After a while, she took it.
Nia had six liters left. We had twenty-four. We put it all together. By nightfall, we were down to twenty-one.

### 4 · Ngày 12: một lối ra
[HUD DAY 12 · p3-10]
On Day 12, Nia showed us a route to the dock. It avoided the front aisles and most of the stockroom corridor.
The walkway followed the east wall all the way to the dock.
[p3-11]
At the end was the maintenance door. The one with the bent hinge. The one I'd forced.
[p3-12]
Maya and I checked the van while Nia waited inside the dock. Half a tank. Three seats.
Maya started it, then cut the engine. Heads turned at the alley mouth.
We hurried back inside.
Next time, all three of us had to be aboard before Maya turned the key.

### 5 · Kế hoạch loa
[p3-13]
Maya had saved the speaker for a reason. Now we had three people to get across that alley.
She wired the front speaker to repeat the greeting, for as long as we held the switch down.
We found two staff radios and put fresh batteries in them.
If we could keep it playing, it might pull them away from the alley.
[p3-14 · loa "Welcome!" 10 giây]
We ran the speaker for ten seconds. Then we cut it.
Several heads in the street turned toward the speaker. On the alley camera, others stayed where they were.
[p3-15]
We'd have to start it well before we moved. And watch the alley until the way was clear.
[p3-16 · HUD WATER 12 L]
The greeting had drawn more of them against the front glass. The crack had spread.
On the morning we left, that glass would have to hold much longer.

### 6 · Ngày 13: đêm cuối
[HUD DAY 13 · p3-17]
On Day 13, we set aside six liters for the drive. The other six had to last all three of us that day. We packed cans, tools, and rope. I kept the steel pipe where I could reach it.
We left almost everything else behind.
[p3-18]
Maya rigged a clamp so the speaker switch could stay pressed without a hand on it.
Speaker on at five thirty. Out of the alley by six. The convoy would leave at seven.
On a clear road, we could get there in fifteen minutes. We had an hour.
[p3-19]
That night, Nia asked Maya if her dad would be at the stadium.
MAYA: I don't know.
Nia waited, but Maya had nothing else to tell her.
Nia looked at her drawings for a long time.
Her dad had told her to wait here. She put on his bakery jacket anyway. Then she packed her bag.
Neither of us told her to hurry.
[p3-20 · HUD WATER 6 L]
Before I slept, I checked the front glass one last time.
There was a new crack. Long. Still growing.

### 7 · Ngày 14: lời chào cuối cùng
[HUD DAY 14 · 05:30 · s01a]
Five thirty. I switched the speaker on.
SPEAKER: Welcome to Corner Fresh!
[p3-21 · loa lặp]
I spent two weeks making that door shut up.
Now I need it to scream.
[p3-22]
Maya watched the alley camera in the stockroom. Nia waited beside her.
For nearly twenty minutes, the alley didn't empty.
[s01c]
I held the switch. The crack in the front glass spread another inch.
Then Maya's voice came through the staff radio. They were finally moving toward the front.
MAYA: Hold it. A few more seconds.
Then the alley was clear. I clamped the switch and went after them.

### 8 · Cửa bảo trì
[05:50 · p3-23]
By five fifty, we were moving along the metal walkway toward the dock. Nia first, then Maya, then me.
[p3-24]
Maya held the sagging door clear of the frame from the loft side. Nia squeezed through into the dock.
I passed the bags through to Nia. Then I crawled into the dock. Maya stayed behind, holding the door.
The bent top hinge tore free before Maya could follow.
[p3-25 · rầm]
The door dropped onto its lower corner, and wedged in the frame.
Maya was on the other side.
[p3-26]
Nia stood beside me in the dock. A few steps to the shutter. The van beyond it.
[p3-27]
I turned away from the shutter, and went back to Maya.
I jammed the pipe under the door and leaned on it. Nothing moved.
I heard the front glass crack again. We were supposed to be driving by six.
I shoved the pipe farther under the door. I pressed down, using the sill for leverage.
Her bandaged hand came through the gap. I pushed down harder on the pipe.
The bottom edge cleared the sill. I pulled the door open. Maya squeezed through.

### 9 · Thoát
[p3-28 · cửa cuốn]
I dragged the pallet jack clear. Then I hauled the shutter up. Metal screamed. At the alley mouth, a head turned.
[p3-29 · máy nổ]
Nia climbed into the middle seat. I got in beside her. She took my hand.
Maya turned the key. In the mirror, I saw a few of them turn toward the engine.
[06:05 · p3-30]

Behind us, the front glass finally gave in.
The carts and shelves held for a moment. Then the crowd pushed them back, and poured into Corner Fresh.
And the speaker was still welcoming every single one of them.

### 10 · Đường tới sân vận động
[p3-32]
Six miles by the main road.
[06:20 · p3-33]
We spent fifteen minutes creeping past abandoned cars. At six twenty, the cars filled every lane. Shapes moved between them.
There was no gap wide enough for the van. Maya turned us around.
The way through the industrial park was longer.
[p3-34]
We cut through the industrial park, between warehouses with their doors torn open.
[06:50 · p3-35]
At six fifty, wrecked cars were tangled in a police barricade across the bridge. Both lanes were blocked.
Maya stopped. I opened the map. Ten minutes until the convoy left. We still had to get across.
The map showed another bridge. I pointed to the turn. Maya took it. The road led away from the stadium.
Seven o'clock passed. We still hadn't reached the other bridge.
We crossed the other bridge. Maya turned back toward the stadium. I kept watching for the gates.

### 11 · Quá muộn
[07:20 · p3-36]
We reached Harbor Stadium at seven twenty.
The gates were shut. The parking lot was empty. The convoy was gone.
For a moment, none of us got out.
[p3-37]
Outside the gates, the registration tent was still standing. Papers covered the ground. A route map was drawn on a whiteboard.
The next stop on the evacuation route. The harbor.
[p3-38 · chữ trên hình: R. OKAFOR — SUNRISE BAKERY — BOARDED]
Next to it was the boarding list. The radio had said families could use those names to find each other.
Nia found it first. R. Okafor. Sunrise Bakery. Her dad. Beside it, one word. Boarded.
He had made it this far. Now we had somewhere to look.
Nia put her finger on her dad's name.
For the first time, she asked us when we were leaving.
