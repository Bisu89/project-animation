# Short 2 - How Hannibal Won by Letting Rome Win

Rome had eighty thousand soldiers at Cannae. Hannibal had forty thousand.

He put his weakest troops in the middle, in a crescent that bulged toward the Romans.

The Romans saw a weak center. So they pushed.

The center gave way, slowly. The crescent flattened, then curved inward.

On both sides, his best infantry turned in. His cavalry rode around the back.

Eighty thousand Romans were packed so tight they could not lift their arms.

Only the edges could fight.

Rome did not lose because it was small. It lost because it walked into a shape built for it.
