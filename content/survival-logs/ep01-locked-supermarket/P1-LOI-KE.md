Theo P1-CANH.md v2 (đã duyệt). Bản 2 sửa theo review Codex của bản 1 (8/10; 43 câu sửa). Giọng Titan, eleven_multilingual_v2 (không
tag cảm xúc; nhịp bằng dấu chấm, xuống dòng; "..." chỉ khi do dự/chờ nghe).
Quy ước: dòng thường = lời kể. `MAYA:` / `NEWS:` / `SPEAKER:` = thoại giọng khác. `[...]` = âm thanh/hình/chữ trên hình, không đọc.
Gửi TTS: mỗi lượt giọng xuất riêng, bỏ nhãn và ghi chú; chỗ cắt giữa từ ("Wel—", bản tin bị cắt) xử lý bằng dựng âm thanh.

**Đổi giờ so với P1-CANH v2:** cảnh 6A 45→50 s, cảnh 12 75→80 s, cảnh 17 25→15 s (tổng vẫn 13:30).

**Giọng phụ:** MAYA: nữ trưởng thành, trung trầm, giọng Mỹ tự nhiên, khác rõ Titan (13 khàn, kiệt sức; 14 dè chừng; 15 giận có kiểm
soát; hook qua bộ đàm: ngắn, tập trung). NEWS: người đọc tin Mỹ, rõ, đều, tiết chế + chất radio khi dựng (không để nhiễu che luật 24
giờ). SPEAKER: giọng quảng cáo bán lẻ sáng, vui vừa, hơi máy móc; **mọi lần Welcome dùng cùng một bản ghi**.

### 1 · 0:00–0:25 · Mở lạnh, ngày 14
[Loa lặp liên tục, kính rên, vết nứt cũ lan ra]
SPEAKER: Welcome to Corner Fresh!
MAYA: Hold it. A few more seconds.
My thumb is on the switch.
Hundreds of them are turning toward the speaker.
I spent two weeks making that door shut up.
Now I need it to scream.

### 2 · 0:25–0:55 · Ca đêm
SPEAKER: Welcome to Corner Fresh!
When this started, the worst thing in my life was that speaker.
It welcomed everyone. Customers. Delivery guys. Once, a raccoon.
I worked nights at Corner Fresh.
Six aisles. One self-checkout.
The bread van came every night at one thirty.
[Radio quầy bắt đầu chen vào]

### 3 · 0:55–1:25 · Bản tin
NEWS: Those bitten develop a high fever and turn within twenty-four hours. Stay indoors. Do not approach.
[Cắt tiếng radio khi dựng]
I thought it was a riot. Some city thing that would be over by morning.
Then my only customer dropped her basket and ran.
I'd never seen her run before.

### 4 · 1:25–1:55 · Hai người bỏ đi
My manager left without a word.
Through the dock window, I watched the bread driver answer his phone.
He listened. Then he looked back at the dock door.
Then he ran toward the main road.
Two people left that night. Only one of them looked back.

### 5 · 1:55–2:35 · Cửa mở
[Im lặng. Một bóng người lê vào vùng cảm biến]
The first one reached the doors.
The sensor picked it up. The doors slid open.
SPEAKER: Welcome to Corner Fresh!
Its head snapped toward the speaker.
And across the street, one by one... the others turned too.
The store did exactly what it was built to do.
It invited them in.

### 6A · 2:35–3:25 · Bốn lần "Welcome"
It got tangled in the carts outside, clear of the door.
I ran for the control box behind the service desk.
First switch. The lights flickered.
SPEAKER: Welcome to Corner Fresh!
Second switch. The checkout rebooted.
[Bíp SCO]
SPEAKER: Welcome to Corner Fresh!
Third switch.
SPEAKER: Wel—
The motor cut out.
The emergency closer pulled the doors shut.
I locked it by hand.
Its face pressed against the glass.
Four "Welcomes."
That's how long it took me to find the right switch.

### 6B · 3:25–4:05 · Cửa sau
Then I remembered. The loading dock. Still open for the bread delivery.
I sprinted to the back and dragged the shutter down. It screamed the whole way, and I hated it for every inch.
On my way back through the stockroom corridor, my shoulder caught a rolling shelf.
It tipped, and crashed against something near the office.
I didn't stop to check what it hit.
That's the part I still think about.

### 7 · 4:05–4:35 · Đêm 1
That night, I sat in the stockroom holding a steel pipe.
Something fell behind the blocked corridor.
I said, "Hello?"
Nobody answered.
I silenced my phone, shut off the radio, and pulled the batteries from the toys.
I told myself it was a box.
I needed it to be a box.
[Dòng nhật ký trên hình: Rule #1: Silence everything you don't need.]

### 8 · 4:35–5:15 · Ngày 2: mất điện
[HUD: DAY 2]
On Day 2, the power died.
The exit signs stayed on. So did the self-checkout.
Both were on backup power.
The fridges went quiet. When the power dies, a supermarket becomes a clock.
So I ate the bread and fruit that were still good, and saved the cans for later.
I wasn't risking the milk or meat once the fridges warmed up.
I switched off the camera recorder and the monitors, too. Every watt counted.

### 9 · 5:15–6:05 · Nước và toilet
[HUD: WATER 117 L]
That morning, I counted the bottled water.
About a hundred and twenty liters.
That afternoon, the tap coughed, hissed, and stopped.
A hundred and twenty liters sounds like a lot... until the toilets stop flushing.
I needed about four liters a day for drinking and basic hygiene.
More if I got sick, or the store got hot.
I lined a lidded bucket with trash bags and added cat litter.
I kept it away from the food, with soap and a little water for my hands.
I wasn't wasting drinking water on a flush.

### 10 · 6:05–6:45 · Ánh sáng
I cleared the windows and covered the lower glass with cardboard.
The carts and shelves went about three feet back.
That night I was working with a flashlight near the front. A thin line of light slipped past the edge of the cardboard.
A shape pressed against the glass. Right where the light was.
I clicked it off and didn't breathe.
[Vài giây chỉ có tiếng cào kính]
It didn't hear me.
It saw the light.
[Dòng nhật ký trên hình: Light is an invitation.]

### 11 · 6:45–7:30 · Bíp đầu tiên
Earlier that day, my second count came up one pack of crackers short.
Probably I counted wrong.
Later that night, I heard one short beep from the front.
The self-checkout.
I crept out with the pipe. The screen was back on its welcome page. No one there.
Probably the machine glitched.
I went back to the stockroom and wedged a pallet against the double doors.
I was getting very good at "probably."

### 12 · 7:30–8:50 · Ngày 3: cửa sau
[HUD: DAY 3]
On Day 3, I decided I couldn't hide in a supermarket forever.
The bread van was still in the alley. Nothing near the cab.
I cleared my way back and left the pallet jack beside the shutter.
Close enough to grab. Clear of the opening.
I lifted the shutter halfway.
Metal on metal. At the end of the alley, one of them turned its head.
I reached the van. The cab was locked.
Then two more came around the corner.
I dropped my tool bag and ran. Ducked under, dragged the shutter down, jammed the pallet jack against it.
Something hit the metal right behind my back.
For a long time, I just sat there, breathing.
I still needed the van key.
And a clear way to the cab.
That night, two four-packs of double A batteries were missing.
[HUD: VAN KEY — MISSING]

### 13 · 8:50–10:10 · Ngày 4: người sau bức tường
[HUD: DAY 4]
On Day 4, I finally moved the shelf to reach the cleaning supplies.
I unloaded it, then dragged it clear.
Then I heard a cough. And a weak knock.
MAYA: Please... the shelf. I can't move it.
The office door was open a crack. Behind it was a woman with a bandaged hand.
Her name was Maya.
She'd worked on our doors and speaker.
She said she'd run in through the dock on the first night.
My shelf had hit the office door.
The door had knocked her into the desk.
She'd had water, snacks, and a tiny washroom in there.
She'd tried calling. Tried the high window that morning.
That's where she'd cut her hand.
She'd been sixty feet away.
The whole time.
Then she held up a set of keys.
[Tag chìa trên hình: SUNRISE BAKERY]
She'd found them at the dock, where the driver dropped them.
I looked at the keys.
Then I looked at her hand.

### 14 · 10:10–11:10 · Thỏa thuận
[Chữ trên hình: DAY 4 · 3:00 P.M.]
MAYA: It's glass. I swear it's glass.
I believed her. Mostly.
But the news said twenty-four hours. Fever first, then... the rest.
We agreed she'd stay in the office until three the next afternoon.
Twenty-four hours. I'd check on her every few hours.
If a fever started, we'd know.
She handed me the keys so I could keep them safe while she waited.
It sounded fair when I said it out loud.
That night, I waited until she was asleep.
Then I slid the outside latch shut.
[Tiếng chốt 'cạch' riêng, im]
We agreed on twenty-four hours.
I added the latch on my own.

### 15 · 11:10–12:15 · Ngày 5: chốt cửa
[HUD: DAY 5]
At three o'clock on Day 5, she had no fever. No shaking. Nothing.
I opened the door. She looked at the latch. Then at me.
MAYA: I heard that last night.
MAYA: I agreed to stay. I didn't agree to let you decide when I get out.
She didn't leave. There was nowhere to go.
She set one rule.
MAYA: From now on, we make those calls together.
I said yes, and I gave her the keys back.
[HUD: VAN KEY — FOUND]
That evening, she showed me the backup circuits.
[Hình: Maya dừng lại trước hộp điều khiển loa lâu hơn một nhịp]
I told myself the latch kept us safe.
It only kept me comfortable.

### 16 · 12:15–13:15 · Đêm 5: phiếu
That night, we were both in the stockroom when it came again.
[Bíp SCO, nhỏ, xa]
We ran. The checkout screen had already gone back to its welcome page. No one was there.
But a receipt was waiting in the tray.
[Phiếu: CORNER FRESH — SELF CHECKOUT · 03:12 · CRACKERS × 1 · AA BATTERIES (4-PACK) × 2 · PAYMENT PENDING — 03:12 rõ nhất]
It listed the same things I'd already counted missing.
MAYA: I was with you.
I checked every outside door.
Nothing had been forced.
Maya was right beside me. Every door was locked.
So who was shopping in my store?
