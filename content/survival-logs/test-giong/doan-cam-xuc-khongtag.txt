Three twelve a.m. The machine beeped again. Somebody was standing at the self-checkout... and I was too scared to look. Then the glass groaned. Not a crack. A groan. Like the whole storefront was breathing in. I grabbed the pipe, I grabbed the flashlight, and I ran. Later, I opened the office door and Maya just looked at me. She had heard the latch. Every night. I told myself I locked it to keep us safe. The truth is... I locked it because I was afraid. Fear makes you cruel.
